# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone participating in this project is expected to be respectful,
constructive, and professional.

## Standards

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Critique code, never people.
- Keep discussions on-topic and technical.

## Enforcement

Report violations to **william.phetsinorath@shikanime.studio**. Maintainers
may remove comments, contributions, or participants who violate this conduct.
